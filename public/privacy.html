Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

1. Overview

Profile Optimizer is a career development tool designed to help professionals audit, structure, and maintain their LinkedIn profiles. We respect your data privacy and handle all information in accordance with platform security standards.

2. Information We Collect

  • Basic OpenID profile identity (Member name, profile picture URL, and verified email address) via official LinkedIn OAuth 2.0.
  • Career profile details provided by you (Target role, industry, headline, summary narrative, skills, and work history).
  • Job descriptions that you paste for keyword comparison and ATS relevance scoring.

3. How We Use Information

  • To generate grounded recommendations for your headline, summary, and experience sections based solely on your verified accomplishments.
  • To calculate keyword frequency, detect keyword stuffing, and compute ATS alignment metrics.
  • To draft professional engineering posts for your explicit review and approval.

4. Storage & Security

All OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption. Sensitive credentials are never exposed to browser scripts, logged to server consoles, or shared with unauthorized third parties.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You maintain complete control over your records. You can disconnect your LinkedIn account or permanently purge all stored profiles, revisions, drafs, and integration records at any time via the Purge All User Data button in the settings dashboard.
